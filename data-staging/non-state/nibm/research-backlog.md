### **C2. Remaining Faculties for Subsequent Research Requests**

While the School of Computing has been thoroughly catalogued in this report, NIBM's remaining seven faculties and specialized centers require dedicated programme extraction in subsequent research passes1. Across the institution, these remaining units comprise the School of Business (from which 4 programmes are documented in Table A2, leaving 5 Coventry University undergraduate degrees and multiple feeder Advanced Diplomas and Diplomas in management, human resources, marketing, finance, digital banking, and logistics to be extracted), the School of Engineering (with 3 quantity surveying and electro-mechanical engineering programmes discovered on the SOCE portal in Table C1 and additional engineering degrees pending), the School of Language (from which 1 degree is documented in Table A2, with the BA (Hons) in English Studies and standalone language diplomas pending), the School of Design (with 2 programmes discovered in Table C1), the School of Humanities (with 2 degrees in psychology and multimedia journalism discovered in Table C1), the Business Analytics Center (from which the BSc (Hons) in Data Science is documented in Table A2, leaving the Foundation Course in Data Science and standalone diploma pages pending), and the Productivity & Quality Center (which is listed across NIBM's Degree, HND, Advanced Diploma, and Foundation directories)1.
