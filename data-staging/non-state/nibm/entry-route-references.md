# Supplementary entry routes from supplied report

 Partially Truncated Entry Criteria and Preliminary Entry Routes**

On several NIBM programme pages, the primary G.C.E. A/L or O/L entry option text is partially truncated in the web page preview blocks, whereas the alternative O/L and NIBM Foundation/Certificate entry options are stated in full6. Specifically, on the pages for BSc (Hons) Computer Science with Data Science14 and BSc (Hons) Computer Science with Applied Artificial Intelligence24, only the O/L Option (having sat for G.C.E. A/L in Mathematics, Science, or Technology with ICT and passed G.C.E. O/L with at least 5 credits including "B" passes for Mathematics and Science/ICT and a "C" pass for English) and the NIBM Certificate Option are displayed in full, although sibling four-year degrees on the identical template specify an A/L Option of at least 2 passes at G.C.E. A/L in Mathematics, Science, or Technology with ICT15. For Diploma in Software Engineering (Full Time)21, Diploma in Network Engineering (Full Time)36, and Diploma in Management Information Systems (Part Time)39, the A/L Option specifies a minimum of 3 passes at G.C.E. A/L, but the remainder of the A/L stream line is truncated in the page summary; prospective students entering solely via A/L passes without relying on the O/L or NIBM Foundation options should therefore confirm stream eligibility with the institution. Similarly, on the Foundation Programme for Bachelor's Degree page under the School of Business, the English language remediation requirement is published in full—stipulating that applicants without a pass in G.C.E. O/L English Language must obtain at least 4.5 in IELTS or complete NIBM's Certificate Course in English Language—while the baseline O/L credit count is omitted6.  
Furthermore, NIBM utilizes four short-term Certificate and Transfer programmes as formal admission pathways ("NIBM Option") into its Diplomas and Undergraduate Degrees11.
